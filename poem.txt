Whispers of the Wind

Gentle breeze, a soft caress,
Nature's voice, so effortless.
Through leaves and grass, it softly sings,
A lullaby of forgotten things.

In meadows wide and forests deep,
The wind its secrets still does keep.
A timeless dance of air in flight,
Bringing day and ushering night.

So listen close when zephyrs blow,
For stories old and truths to know.
In whispers of the wind, you'll find
The endless wonders of the mind.
