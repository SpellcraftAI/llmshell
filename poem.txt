Whispers of the Wind

Gentle breeze, a soft caress,
Nature's voice, so effortless.
Through leaves and grass, it softly sings,
A lullaby of forgotten things.

Dancing flowers, swaying trees,
Secrets carried on the breeze.
In whispers soft, in whispers low,
The wind tells tales of long ago.

Listen close, with heart and soul,
To stories that make us whole.
For in the wind's ethereal song,
We find where we truly belong. soothing charms.
