The Valiant Knight

In armor bright, a noble sight,
A knight stands tall and true.
With sword in hand, to guard the land,
His heart both brave and true.

Through forests deep and mountains steep,
He rides on trusty steed.
To right the wrongs, and sing the songs,
Of chivalry and deed.

In castle halls and tournament calls,
His honor put to test.
With lance held high against the sky,
He strives to be the best.

For king and crown, of great renown,
He pledges life and more.
A beacon bright in darkest night,
The knight we all adore.
