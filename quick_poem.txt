Whispers of the Terminal

In the glow of the screen so bright,
Words appear, a digital sight.
A poem born in circuits and code,
A creative spark, swiftly showed.

Cursor blinking, thoughts unfold,
A story in characters, swiftly told.
In this realm of ones and zeros,
Imagination becomes our heroes.

Swift as light, the verses flow,
In the terminal, our art does grow.
A moment's inspiration, forever kept,
In this file where creativity leapt.
