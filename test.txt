Whispers of the Wind

Gentle breeze, a soft caress,
Nature's voice, so effortless.
Rustling leaves, a sweet refrain,
Whispers secrets, joy and pain.

Through meadows green and forests deep,
The wind does dance, and secrets keep.
It carries dreams on gossamer wings,
And in the air, it softly sings.

A timeless force, both wild and free,
Connecting earth, and you, and me.
In whispers of the wind, we find
The breath of life for all mankind.
