Whispers of the Wind
xyz
Nature's voice, so effortless.
Rustling leaves, a sweet refrain,
Whispers secrets, joy and pain.

Dancing branches, swaying free,
Tell tales of what used to be.
In the wind, we find our song,
Carried forth, where we belong.

Invisible force, yet so strong,
Guiding us where we belong.
In its breath, we find our way,
Through the night and into day.

Whispers of the wind, so clear,
Remind us why we're here.
To dance, to sing, to love, to grow,
In the gentle winds that blow.
