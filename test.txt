Metamorphosis

In the garden of life, we plant our seeds,
Nurtured by time, watered by deeds.
Each day a petal, each year a leaf,
Growing through joy, and sometimes grief.

From tender sprout to mighty tree,
We stretch our branches, wild and free.
Roots deep in earth, crown touching sky,
A living testament of you and I.

Through seasons changing, we adapt and thrive,
In nature's dance, we come alive.
From caterpillar to butterfly's wing,
We emerge renewed, ready to sing.

So embrace the change, let yourself grow,
For in transformation, your true self you'll know.

In every change, a lesson learned,
In every shift, a page is turned.
Your metamorphosis is always near.

With every breath, a chance to grow,
In change, we find our inner glow.
The journey never truly ends,
As life itself forever transcends.
Your metamorphosis is always near.
